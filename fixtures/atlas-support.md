# Atlas Support Handbook

## Incident priorities

Atlas classifies a complete production outage or a confirmed security breach as
a priority-one (P1) incident. The support team acknowledges a P1 incident within
15 minutes, provides status updates every 30 minutes, and keeps an incident
commander assigned until service is restored.

A priority-two (P2) incident affects an important feature but has a practical
workaround. The acknowledgement target for P2 incidents is four business hours.

## Escalation

Customers open incidents in the support portal. For P1 incidents, they should
also call the emergency number listed in their service agreement. The incident
commander coordinates engineering, customer communication, and the final review.
